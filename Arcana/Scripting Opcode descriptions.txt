	     then stores the result in that RAM address.
	(2b) RAM address
	(1b) Function to perform
	0: AND	1: OR	2: ADC	3: EOR
	(2b) Operand/comparator
15: (1,1,2b) Same as 0E, but compares one of $09A3,x, $09C7,x, $09EB,x, $0A0F,x with a value.
	(1b) RAM address (actor ID)

	15 01: Load $09C7,x
	15 02: Load $09EB,x
	15 03: Load $0A0F,x
	(1b) Function to perform
	0: AND	1: OR	2: ADC	3: EOR
	(2b) Operand/comparator
